    What it checks

    Four checks on the front of the card. Each is scored on its own, and the results show which one is holding the grade back.

    • CenteringBorder ratiosLeft/right and top/bottom, measured on a flat 63 × 88 mm card.
    • CornersWhitening and roundingCompared with the border colour beside each corner, so uneven light is not read as wear.
    • EdgesChippingEach edge is scanned for light flecks and the marks are drawn on the card.
    • SurfaceSpecks and grimeThe border is checked for specks. The optional AI check looks at the whole face.

    Know the limits

    • Not an official grade. No grading company has reviewed these numbers.
    • Front of the card only. Back centering is not measured yet.
    • Photos can mislead. Glare, flash and soft focus read as wear, and some flaws do not show in a photo.
    • Wear thresholds are early. They still need checking against cards with known grades.
    • Standard bordered cards work best. For full-art cards, place the border lines by hand.

    Questions

    Do I need an account?

    No. There is no sign-up and Holo Grade stores nothing about you or your cards.

    Where does my photo go?

    Finding the card and measuring it happen in your browser. If the AI option is on, the straightened card image is sent to an AI model to read the name and check condition. Turn it off to measure only.

    Is it free?

    Yes. Measuring happens in your browser and the AI card reading is free.

    How accurate is it?

    Border measurements are good to roughly 2% on a sharp, square-on photo. Wear detection is newer, so treat it as a guide and compare it with the real card.

          How this works and where it falls short
          • Finding the card, measuring borders and checking wear all happen on your device. Reading the card and the AI condition check send the straightened card image to an AI model.
          • Measured wear covers whitening on corners and edges and specks on the border. It does not see grime, creases or scratches on the card face. The AI condition check looks for those and can only lower the ceiling.
          • Every number is an estimate from a photo, not a grade. Glare, flash and soft focus can fool it. Compare against the real card.
          • Corner detection works best on a plain, dark surface with nothing overlapping the edges. Expect a few pixels of error, so zoom in with the rings and fine-tune.
          • Border detection expects a standard bordered card. For full-art cards, place the lines by hand.
          • The corner, edge and surface thresholds are the page's own starting guesses and have not been checked against cards with known grades.
